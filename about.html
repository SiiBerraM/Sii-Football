About Sii-Football

Created and maintained by Berra in Casablanca, Morocco. Sii-Football is an open global sports data API with dedicated coverage for para-sports alongside mainstream and international sports.

AdvertisementSponsor / Ad Space

Mission & Global Sports Coverage

  • Spotlight para-sports (Amputee Football, Blind Football, CP Football, and more) with structured public data.
  • Expand across all global sports and competitions so any developer or app can query sports information in one place.

In Partnership with SiiMatchMind

SiiMatchMind has full, unrestricted permission to fetch and consume all data and endpoints published by Sii-Football without restrictions or conditions.
